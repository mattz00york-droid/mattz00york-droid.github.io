I Short You

Creepy stories in 60 seconds. A new part every day.

I Short You is a faceless short-video channel on YouTube Shorts and TikTok. Each story runs as a series of ten episodes told with narration, illustrated comic-style characters and original music. All stories are fiction.

I Short You Publisher is the small private tool the channel uses to upload its own videos to its own YouTube and TikTok accounts. It is not offered to the public and does not sign in other people.
