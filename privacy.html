Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

This policy covers I Short You Publisher (“the app”), a private tool that uploads videos made by the I Short You channel to the channel's own YouTube and TikTok accounts.

What data the app uses

  • Google / YouTube: the app asks only for the youtube.upload permission, which lets it upload videos to the authorized channel. It does not read your videos, comments, subscribers, email or any other data.
  • TikTok: the app asks for user.info.basic (your open ID, display name and avatar, used only to confirm which account is connected) and video.publish (to post videos to that account).

How it is stored

Access tokens are stored only on the channel owner's own computer, in files readable only by that user. They are never sent anywhere except back to Google or TikTok to upload videos. The app has no server and no database.

Sharing

The app does not sell, share, transfer or disclose any user data to anyone. It does not use data for advertising, profiling or AI training.

Google API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access and deleting data

You can revoke the app's access at any time from Google Account permissions or, on TikTok, under Settings › Security › Apps and services. Deleting the stored token files on the owner's computer removes all data the app holds.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and is used only by the channel owner.

Contact

Questions about this policy: message the I Short You channel on YouTube or TikTok.
